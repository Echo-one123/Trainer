# 18th Edition Trainer

Exam trainer for the City & Guilds / EAL 18th Edition (BS 7671:2018+A4:2026) exam: 859 questions, book-order exams matching the EAL question split, smart exams aimed at weak areas, a progress tracker and tips on where answers went wrong.

Open it at **https://echo-one123.github.io/trainer/** — on an iPhone, open in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen.

Progress is saved in the browser on the device you use.
